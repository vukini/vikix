# Lisbon to Porto by bike

Six days, 320 km, along the coast on the EuroVelo 1 route. Mostly flat,
with a headwind from the north most afternoons: ride in the mornings.

Packing: two panniers, a spare tube, a pump, a rain jacket, lights (the
tunnels near Nazaré are dark). Trains back from Porto take bikes for 5 €.
