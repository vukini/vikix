# Sourdough

## The starter

Feed it once a day at room temperature: 50 g starter, 50 g flour, 50 g
water. It's ready when it has doubled and a spoonful floats.

## The loaf

500 g strong white flour, 350 g water, 100 g starter, 10 g salt. Fold it
four times, half an hour apart, then leave it in the fridge overnight.
Bake at 240 °C in a lidded pot: 20 minutes with the lid, 25 without.
