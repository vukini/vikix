Music plays a pattern in a separate Lisp process. The quiet bar field samples its beat and tempo about once a second; it is not a flashing metronome or an audio latency measurement. While recording it names the active source in alert colour. Open Music controls for playback and explicit recording; the browser also holds the preserved capture inbox.
