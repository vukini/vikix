Focus time: so many minutes with do not disturb on by itself, then a break. "focus 24" is the minutes left while you work; notifications wait meanwhile. "break 5", in the accent colour, is the break after it. Super+m, Notifications, starts 25 or 50 minutes and stops what is on; a rule can start one at a time of day.
