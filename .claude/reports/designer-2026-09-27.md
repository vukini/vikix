Reviewed: development checkout `VERSION` 0.21.1; the running desktop (`~/vikix/VERSION`) is also 0.21.1.

## 1. StumpWM draws in the X bitmap font `9x15`, not Iosevka (the biggest consistency problem)

**What I saw.** A query of the running WM gives `(screen-font (current-screen))` = `XLIB:FONT "9x15"`. Nothing in `config/stumpwm/` sets a font. So the bar, the Vikix menu (Super+m), the key help (Super+F1), messages and the input prompt all use the X core fallback font. Everything else uses a TrueType font. In `bar-left-zoom.png` the bar's thin bitmap glyphs look nothing like the Iosevka in the terminal right below it. The window title `0*? Next feature` shows a `?` because `9x15` has no glyph for the symbol that program puts in its title (the `✳`). `^B` in the group list (`vikix-mode-line-groups`) doesn't make anything bold either: in StumpWM `^B` means "bright colours", which only affects the indexed colours `^0`–`^7`. It has no effect with hex colours (see `stumpwm/color.lisp`, line 30).

**Why it matters.** The window manager's own surfaces are the ones you see most (the bar all day, the menu and help on every lookup). They are the only part that doesn't share the family, and non-ASCII titles break.

**Fix.** In `config/stumpwm/vikix/theme.lisp`, in the "Shape, not colour" section, load the contrib `ttf-fonts` module and set Iosevka, falling back to `9x15` if anything fails:

```lisp
;; Iosevka, like the terminal, rofi and dunst. ttf-fonts needs clx-truetype
;; (Quicklisp's truetype-clx) in the image; without it keep the X font.
(handler-case
    (progn
      (load-module "ttf-fonts")
      (setf (symbol-value (find-symbol "*FONT-DIRS*" :xft)) (list #p"/usr/share/fonts/"))
      (funcall (find-symbol "CACHE-FONTS" :xft))
      (set-font (make-instance (find-symbol "FONT" :xft)
                               :family "Iosevka" :subfamily "Regular"
                               :size 11 :antialias t)))
  (error (e) (message "^1Vikix: Iosevka not loaded, keeping 9x15:^n ~a" e)))
```

Check this in `void-vm` before shipping. On Void, `font-iosevka` ships only `.ttc` collections (`/usr/share/fonts/TTF/Iosevka.ttc`), and I couldn't confirm that `zpb-ttf`/`clx-truetype` read collections. If they don't, use a single-file `.ttf` (for example a separately installed Iosevka TTF) or DejaVu Sans Mono (already installed), which still beats `9x15`. `lib/build-stumpwm.lisp` should `ql:quickload "clx-truetype"` into the image so the module loads offline. Once this is done, drop the no-op `^B`/`^b` in `vikix-mode-line-groups` (the brackets already mark the current group) and fix the comment "the current one in bold".

## 2. On this machine the picom config makes the bar and unfocused windows see-through

**What I saw.** `~/.config/picom/picom.conf` is not Vikix's starter. It is an older personal config (glx, `inactive-opacity = 0.83`, gaussian blur, 8 px `corner-radius`, shadows, fading). Effects in `current-void.png`:
- The bar should be `#1e1e2e`, but samples show `#303749` and `#233446`: the wallpaper tints through. It also has rounded corners (visible at its bottom-left in `bar-left-zoom.png`), although it is a full-width strip.
- The two unfocused terminals on the right show the wallpaper's orange behind their text (sampled `#433834` where the background should be `#1e1e2e`). fg on that patch is 7.8:1 instead of 11.3:1. Worse, the background changes across the window, and that is tiring to read even when the ratio passes.
- The rounded corners clash with StumpWM's square 2 px borders, dunst's `corner_radius = 0` and rofi's square window.

**Why it matters.** Vikix's own starter (`config/picom/picom.conf`: xrender, no shadow, no fading, "here to stop tearing, not for effects") is the calm design. The installed config undoes it and makes every theme's colours unreliable.

**Fix.** This is the user's file, so it is their call. The design recommendation is to copy the starter over it, or at least set these in `~/.config/picom/picom.conf`:
```
inactive-opacity = 1.0;
frame-opacity = 1.0;
corner-radius = 0;
blur-method = "none";   # or delete the blur { } block
shadow = false;
fading = false;
```
Nothing to change in the repository.

## 3. The paper theme's terminal colours are too faint on its background

**What I saw (from `themes/paper.theme`, bg `#eff1f5`).**

| colour | hex | on bg |
|---|---|---|
| fg | #4c4f69 | 7.06 |
| subtle | #6c6f85 | **4.37** |
| accent / color4 / color12 | #1e66f5 | **4.34** |
| alert / color1 | #d20f39 | 4.80 |
| color2 green | #40a02b | **2.96** |
| color3 yellow | #df8e1d | **2.31** |
| color5 magenta | #ea76cb | **2.34** |
| color6 cyan | #179299 | **3.31** |
| color7 white | #acb0be | 1.91 (expected for "white" on light) |
| color8 bright black | #6c6f85 | 4.37 |
| color15 | #bcc0cc | 1.61 |

Green, yellow, magenta and cyan are the colours `ls --color`, `git diff`, `git log` (yellow hashes) and prompts use for text, and all four fall below 3.5:1. Yellow and magenta are the worst, at about 2.3:1. The bar's accent-coloured `updates`/`awake` and rofi's prompt are also just under 4.5. The hues themselves are well apart (smallest CIE76 ΔE between colours 1–6 is about 61), so the problem is lightness only.

**Why it matters.** Paper is the daytime theme. In a bright room, low contrast is what makes a light theme tiring.

**Fix.** Darken the text colours while keeping their hues (a revised `themes/paper.theme` is below). Every text colour then reaches at least 4.5:1: green 4.71, yellow 5.09, magenta 5.20, cyan 5.15, blue/accent 5.28, subtle 5.01. The background `#eff1f5` itself is fine for glare: it is not pure white, and fg on it is 7:1.

## 4. The bar's indicators: colour should carry one meaning each

**What I saw (`modeline.lisp`).** The indicators on the right are `rec` (alert), `awake` (accent), `quiet` (alert) and `updates` (accent). Contrast on the bar background:
- void: alert 7.08, accent 7.79. Fine.
- paper: alert 4.80, accent **4.34**.

The meanings are mixed. Red marks both "the screen is being recorded" (a privacy state you must not miss) and "notifications paused" (a mode you chose). Blue marks both "updates are waiting" (something to act on) and "keep awake" (a mode you chose). If `rec` and `quiet` are on together, the only red on the bar is split between two things.

**Fix.** Give each colour one meaning:
- **alert = something is watching or needs you now**: only `rec`.
- **accent = something to act on**: `updates`.
- **subtle = a mode you switched on**: `awake` and `quiet`. They are still visible, with no alarm (void subtle 7.37:1; paper subtle 5.01 with the revision).

In `config/stumpwm/vikix/modeline.lisp`, change `(vikix-colour :alert)` in `vikix-mode-line-quiet` and `(vikix-colour :accent)` in `vikix-mode-line-awake` to `(vikix-colour :subtle)`. `theme.lisp` already reads `:subtle` from the theme files, but the fallback palette in `*vikix-themes*` lacks it, so add `:subtle "#a6adc8"` there too. The order `rec awake quiet updates` is right: the most important comes first.

## 5. Bar: the current window is a bright reversed block, and there is some cryptic text

**What I saw (`bar-left-zoom.png`, `bar-right-zoom.png`).**
- The current window is drawn with `*mode-line-highlight-template*` `^R~A^r`: fg and bg swapped. In void that makes a `#cdd6f4` block, the brightest thing on screen at night.
- `*window-format*` is StumpWM's default `%m%n%s%50t`, which gives `0*? Next feature  1+Alacritty  2-Alacritty`. The `*`, `+` and `-` status marks repeat what the highlight already says.
- On the right: `VID 52%  vol 40%  ~ 100%`. That is three percentages, and only one is labelled. The network gives no hint that it is Wi-Fi, and the battery's `~` (battery-portable's "not charging or discharging") is cryptic.

**Fix.**
- In `vikix-apply-theme` (`config/stumpwm/vikix/theme.lisp`), make the current window accent text instead of a block, so it follows theme changes:
  ```lisp
  (setf *mode-line-highlight-template*
        (format nil "^(:push)^(:fg \"~a\")~~A^(:pop)" (vikix-colour :accent)))
  ```
- In `modeline.lisp`: `(setf *window-format* "%n %30t")`. This also affects StumpWM's window list, which is fine.
- Label consistently with lower-case words, like `vol`. In `bin/vikix-net`, print `wifi VID 52%`, or just the name when the signal is 60% or more, since a strong signal is not news. For the battery, show `bat 84%`, and nothing at all when on AC and full: `*battery-format*`/a small `%B` wrapper in `modeline.lisp`.

## 6. Fonts and sizes differ between programs on this machine

**What I saw.**
- Repo starters: alacritty Iosevka 11.5, rofi Iosevka 12, dunst Iosevka 11.
- Installed user files: `~/.config/rofi/config.rasi` has `font: "mono 12"`, and `~/.config/dunst/dunstrc` has `font = Monospace 10`. Both resolve to DejaVu Sans Mono (`fc-match monospace`). The dunstrc is from before Vikix (Nov 2025) and still uses the deprecated `geometry` key, which dunst 1.13 ignores.

So on this desktop four programs use three fonts: Iosevka (terminal), DejaVu Sans Mono (rofi, dunst) and `9x15` (StumpWM).

**Fix.** These are user files, so this is advice to the user: set `font: "Iosevka 12";` in `~/.config/rofi/config.rasi` and `font = Iosevka 11` in `~/.config/dunst/dunstrc` (and replace `geometry` with `width`/`origin`/`offset` as in `config/dunst/dunstrc`). For the repo, a two-step size scale is enough: 11 for dunst and StumpWM, 11.5–12 for terminal and launcher, as the starters nearly have.

## 7. Selections: three different ideas, and the dark one glares

**What I saw (generated files).**
- alacritty selection and rofi's selected row: accent background with bg text. Void: `#89b4fa` block, 7.79:1. Paper: 4.34:1.
- StumpWM menus (Vikix menu, key help, theme picker): `render-strings` in `stumpwm/color.lisp` fills the selected row with the fg colour and swaps text to bg. In void that is a `#cdd6f4` block, and StumpWM has no setting for it.
- The bar: reversed block (finding 5).

In void, large light-blue or near-white blocks are the harshest thing at night.

**Fix.** Add one theme key, `sel`, a quiet surface colour for selections, and use it in rofi and alacritty:
- void `sel=#45475a` (fg on it 6.31:1)
- paper `sel=#ccd0da` (fg on it 5.17:1)

In `theme_write` in `bin/vikix`:
- alacritty `[colors.selection] background = "$c_sel"`, `text = "CellForeground"`
- rofi `element selected.normal, element selected.active { background-color: @sel; text-color: @fg; }`, plus `prompt { text-color: @accent; }` unchanged, so the accent still marks "where you are"
- kitty `selection_background $c_sel`, `selection_foreground none`

`theme_read` must fall back to `color0` when a user's own theme has no `sel`, so existing themes keep working. StumpWM's menus keep their fg block unless `render-strings` is overridden, which I don't recommend. Their selected row is one line in a small window, so it matters less.

## 8. Dunst: the volume/brightness bar uses dunst's own colour

**What I saw.** `bin/vikix-osd` shows volume as a dunst progress bar (`int:value`). The drop-in `~/.config/dunst/dunstrc.d/10-vikix-theme.conf` sets `frame_color`, `background` and `foreground` but not `highlight`. So the bar is drawn in dunst's built-in highlight colour, not the theme's accent, and it stays the same in paper. I couldn't check this on screen (no notification sent).

**Fix.** In the dunst heredoc of `theme_write` (`bin/vikix`), add:
```
[urgency_low]
    highlight = "$c_subtle"
[urgency_normal]
    highlight = "$c_accent"
[urgency_critical]
    highlight = "$c_alert"
```
The rest of the dunst theme is good: critical is bg + fg with an alert frame rather than a red flood, and low is in subtle.

## 9. Void: comments and hints are too dim

**What I saw (`themes/void.theme`, bg `#1e1e2e`).** fg 11.34, subtle 7.37, accent 7.79, alert 7.08, and every ANSI text colour 1–7 is above 7:1. The palette has no pure white and only pastel colours, so it suits night use. The exception is `dim`/`color8` `#585b70`, at **2.46:1**. `color8` is what shells, editors and `git` use for comments, autosuggestions and hints. `dim` is also rofi's placeholder text ("Type to search").

**Fix.** In `themes/void.theme`, set `color8=#7f849c` (4.44:1, Catppuccin overlay1). It stays clearly quieter than `subtle`, and hints become readable. Keep `dim=#585b70` for unfocused borders, where 2.46:1 is a deliberate "recede". In the rofi block of `theme_write`, use `placeholder-color: @subtle;`, or better, a new `color8`-based value once `sel` exists. The current placeholder is text at 2.46:1.

## 10. Terminal colours: distinct hues, but brights are copies

In both themes `color9`–`color14` equal `color1`–`color6`, so a program that asks for "bright red" versus "red" shows no difference. The hues themselves are distinct:
- void: the closest pairs are red/magenta ΔE 27 and green/cyan ΔE 28, which are near each other but tell apart at text size
- paper: every pair is ΔE ≥ 61

This is a small loss. If you want brights to carry information, make them a step lighter in void (e.g. Catppuccin's newer brights) and a step darker in paper. Otherwise leave it: it is a calm choice.

## 11. Small things that are already right (keep them)

- One 2 px border idea everywhere: StumpWM windows, rofi and dunst frames.
- Focus is accent and unfocused is dim, in the same colours as rofi's frame and dunst's normal frame.
- Spacing is consistent: bar padding 8/4, terminal 8/6, rofi 12 around and 4/8 per row, 2 px gaps.
- The lock screen is a flat bg fill, the calmest option.
- The bar shows only groups in use and hides empty indicators.
