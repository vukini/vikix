The door (2026-10-08, `door.lisp`, `vikix door`, Super+m → Door): an agent's `vikix eval` and the MCP `eval` are read without running and walked against one list before anything runs, and what fails waits for the user. The socket (2026-10-09, `socket.lisp`): every form passes one road, reads are answered while a menu is open, and the door stands in its thread. The count (2026-10-11, `vikix door used`): what agents send and what the door holds, kept with the keys' counts in `~/.local/state/vikix/used` (kind `door`: the names on the list a passing form calls, the name that held a form, the user's run or drop by that name; names only), read out as the names called most, the names held with what was done about each, and the names on the list never called, which is what says whether the list is right.

From a second look on 2026-10-05: one box for everything (the palette, Super+Space: windows, commands, projects, web apps, layouts and programs). What gets used is counted (`vikix used`, `used.lisp`: keys, menu entries, palette picks, typed commands, rules, agents' commands; names and counts only, on this machine). The agents' skill is a short first page and a page a subject (80 KB down to 15 for every session).
