### Speed

91. **A socket of Vikix's own beside Swank** (speed; the IPC of `vikix eval`). Every `vikix eval`, the bar's clicks, `vikix what`, the palette and the MCP server go over TCP to Swank at `127.0.0.1:4004` with `~/.slime-secret`, and wait on the main thread, so a menu open means a timeout (the palette gives it a second and a half). A UNIX socket, `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/vikix.sock` (0600: the file's permission is the authentication, no secret sent), served by a thread of Vikix's (`sb-bsd-sockets`, as `rescue.lisp` keeps its own X connection) that answers reads of Lisp state itself and hands anything else to the main thread with a deadline: no handshake, no port to clash with, and a read-only question answered while a menu is open. `bin/vikix-eval` tries the socket first and falls back to Swank, which stays for SLIME in Emacs and `vikix rescue`.
