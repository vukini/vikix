5. **Night light** (gammastep or redshift).
6. **Emoji picker and calculator in rofi.**
7. **A default wallpaper and a picker**, and a wallpaper per theme.
8. **Printing** (CUPS).

10. **A short power menu on its own key.** Suspend, reboot and power off sit at the bottom of a 25-entry menu.
